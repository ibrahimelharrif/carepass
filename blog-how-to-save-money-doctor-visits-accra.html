How to Save Money on Doctor Visits in Accra

August 2026·6 min read

A single doctor's visit in Accra can cost anywhere from GH₵50 at a public health centre to GH₵300 or more at a private specialist clinic. For most families, that range matters a lot. Here are practical ways to reduce what you pay without compromising on the quality of care you receive.

1. Know the difference between a GP and a specialist

Not every health concern needs a specialist. General practitioners are trained to handle the vast majority of common complaints — colds, minor infections, routine check-ups — at a fraction of specialist consultation fees. Save the specialist visit for when your GP actually refers you to one.

2. Ask about consultation fees before you sit down

It sounds obvious, but many people don't ask. Consultation fees vary significantly even between clinics in the same neighbourhood. A quick phone call to the front desk before you go can save you an unpleasant surprise at checkout.

3. Use a health discount card at partner facilities

This is one of the most direct ways to cut costs. A card like CarePass gives you up to 40% off doctor consultations at partner hospitals and clinics across Accra — no waiting period, no paperwork, just a lower price at checkout.

Quick example: a GH₵150 consultation at a partner clinic becomes GH₵90 with a 40% CarePass discount — a GH₵60 saving on a single visit.

4. Take advantage of free monthly health checks

Some healthcare programs, including CarePass, offer subscribers a free blood pressure check and a free random blood glucose test once a month at any one participating provider. These simple checks can catch problems early, long before they turn into a much more expensive visit.

5. Don't skip follow-ups because of cost

It's tempting to skip a follow-up visit to save money, but this often backfires. An untreated or poorly managed condition tends to cost more in the long run — more medication, more tests, sometimes a hospital admission that could have been avoided. If cost is the barrier, look for facilities in your discount network before skipping care altogether.

6. Compare, don't assume

Price doesn't always track with quality in a simple way. A well-regarded clinic might actually charge less than a flashier one down the road. Before committing to a regular provider, it's worth comparing two or three options for both price and patient reviews.

Putting it together

None of these tips require sacrificing the quality of care you receive. They just require a bit more information before you walk into a consultation room — and, where available, a discount card that turns "up to 40% off" into real cedis back in your pocket.
