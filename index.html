About CarePass

A smarter, easier way to access healthcare

CarePass is a digital healthcare platform designed to make quality medical services more accessible and affordable. Through a simple digital health card, CarePass connects you to a trusted network of clinics, hospitals, pharmacies and laboratories, giving you up to 40% discounts on a wide range of healthcare services.

Terms & conditions

The essentials, in plain terms

CarePass is operated by Lakeside University Hospital LTD. Full terms below — please read before subscribing.

01Service definition

CarePass is a digital intermediary platform that enables subscribers holding valid CarePass cards to access medical discounts and benefits through a network of partner healthcare providers — hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and laboratories.

02Subscription & card usage

You agree to pay the applicable subscription fee through the app to activate membership and issue a CarePass card, digital or physical.

The subscription is personal and exclusive to the cardholder and may not be transferred or used by any third party.

03Payment to providers

Direct payment: pay the cost of medical services, after the discount, directly to the healthcare provider in cash or via any method the provider accepts.

Immediate payment: payment is due immediately on receiving the service. Neither CarePass nor providers offer deferred payment or installment options.

Financial liability: CarePass does not act as a financial intermediary and bears no responsibility for transactions between patient and provider.

04Card verification

To benefit from discounts, you must provide valid proof of your CarePass card through an approved verification channel — physical card, app, or website. No discount applies unless verification is successful.

Any attempt to forge or misuse the card results in immediate account suspension and potential legal action.

05Periodic free services

Subscribers receive certain free services — such as blood pressure measurement and random blood glucose testing — once per month, from any one participating provider.

If a free service is unavailable at a given provider, you may be referred to another provider, or directed to support to help locate the nearest one.

06Discount display & calculation

Base prices vary between providers, so CarePass displays discount percentages only within the app, not actual service prices.

The displayed percentage is applied to the provider's standard listed price at the time of service.
